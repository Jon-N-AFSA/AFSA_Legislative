Landmark Wins, 2022–2026

Every year of this window produced documented gains for the enlisted force and their families.

2022
Honoring Our PACT Act Signed Into Law
  • Toxic exposure care expanded — the largest VA health care and benefits expansion in decades
  • New presumptive conditions for burn pit and airborne-hazard exposure

Basic Needs Allowance Takes Effect
  • New allowance to lift junior enlisted families out of food insecurity

  • Veterans Benefits Continuity & Accountability supplemental appropriations secured

  • Major family and quality-of-life improvements carried in the NDAA
